Import activities

Files are read right here in your browser — nothing is uploaded to Strava, Garmin or anyone else.

Getting your whole Strava history
  1. On strava.com, open Settings → My Account.
  2. Under Download or Delete Your Account, click Get Started, then Request Your Archive.
  3. Strava emails you a download link (usually within a few hours).
  4. Drop the downloaded .zip here as-is — no need to unzip it.

Just one hike? On its Strava page, open the ⋯ menu and choose Export GPX or Export Original.

Getting your whole Garmin history
  1. Sign in to your Garmin account and open Account → Data Management.
  2. Choose Export Your Data and request the export.
  3. Garmin emails you a download link once it's ready (this can take a few days).
  4. Drop the downloaded .zip here as-is.

Just one hike? In Garmin Connect, open the activity, click the gear icon and choose Export Original or Export to GPX.

Straight from your watch or phone

Plug a Garmin watch into your computer with its USB cable — it shows up like a USB stick. Your recordings are the .fit files in GARMIN/Activity; select them and drop them here.

Recording with a phone app (Komoot, AllTrails, Gaia GPS, OsmAnd…)? Use its GPX export and drop the file here.
