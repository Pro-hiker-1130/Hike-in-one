# Catalog candidates (maintained by the owner)

A long list of popular hiking gear, grouped by category, for the catalog
bot to research. **The bot never edits this file.** It skips anything that
is already in the catalog (under any spelling or size) or listed under
"Skipped" in `backlog.md`, and records its own progress in `backlog.md`.
Each run it takes from the categories with the fewest catalog products
first, at most 5 per category.
